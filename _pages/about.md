I am an M.Sc. student in Computer Engineering at Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic), preparing PhD applications in distributed systems and edge computing. My thesis, supervised by Dr. Seyyed Ahmad Javadi, is _A Proactive Container-based Auto-scaling Approach for a Hierarchical Orchestration Framework for Edge Computing_. The defence is scheduled for 19 October 2026. It is a three-rung proactive scaler for containerized edge microservices: resize a running replica, add a local replica, then borrow one from a neighboring cluster in the same vicinity. A per-service temporal convolutional network, with a confidence gate, drives that ladder. The cloud is not on the path of a scaling decision.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science from IK International University (2017–2022). My bachelor's project, QuestionParty, was a cross-platform quiz application supervised by Dr. Ali Khaleghi. I continued that system as a software engineering intern at DiSL (Data in Science Laboratory), where I built the Flask and MongoDB backend, the Flutter clients, and the Docker deployment.

At Amirkabir I have been a teaching assistant for Applied Machine Learning, Distributed Systems, and Logic Circuit Design, and I gave a seminar on recurrent neural networks and LSTMs. After the fourth semester, in July 2025, I ranked 2nd among M.Sc. students in the Department of Computer Engineering in the cohort that entered in 2023. I also placed in the top 1% of Iran's M.Sc. university entrance exam.

Before graduate school I studied computer networks (Network+, CCNA, and MCSA) and built mobile and web applications. I am applying to PhD programs on resource management for distributed and cloud-native systems.
