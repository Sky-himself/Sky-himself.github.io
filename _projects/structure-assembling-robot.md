Cross-disciplinary prototype of a structure-assembling robot. I engineered the microcontroller logic and a native Android application that drives the machine over Bluetooth.

That prototype is the subject of US patent application [US20240084610A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240084610A1) ("Structure assembling robot"). The listed inventors are Seyed Kambiz Ghaemi Osguoie and Amin Mostafavi. My contribution, as stated on my CV, is the embedded logic and the Android controller, not inventorship of the application.
