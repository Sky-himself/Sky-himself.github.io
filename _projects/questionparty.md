QuestionParty is an online quiz that runs on mobile and on the web. It was my B.Sc. project at IK International University, supervised by Dr. Ali Khaleghi, and the system I continued as a software engineering intern at DiSL (Data in Science Laboratory) from February 2022 to March 2023.

I was the core developer. The backend is a Python Flask service with Socket.IO for real-time play, Flask-Login for sessions, and MongoDB through PyMongo. The clients are Flutter and Flutter Web. Deployment used Docker, Nginx, uWSGI, and Linux.
