A cross-platform Flutter application for building product catalogues. A user keeps one or more projects, adds products with details and photos from the gallery or the camera, and exports a PDF. I built it in the winter of 2021–2022 after watching a company assemble catalogues by hand across several tools.

Code: [catalogue_maker](https://github.com/Sky-himself/catalogue_maker).
